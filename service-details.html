Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our legal translation services

Are your legal translations court-approved?

Yes, our legal translations are court-approved and accepted by judicial systems worldwide. We provide certified translations with proper notarization and apostille services when required.

How do you ensure confidentiality of legal documents?

We use 256-bit encryption for all file transfers, require NDAs from all translators, and maintain secure servers. All legal documents are handled with the utmost confidentiality and comply with attorney-client privilege standards.

What types of legal documents do you translate?

We translate all types of legal documents including contracts, agreements, court filings, immigration papers, patents, regulatory compliance documents, wills, trusts, power of attorney, and corporate legal documents.

Do you provide notarization and apostille services?

Yes, we provide notarization and apostille services for legal translations that require official certification. We work with certified notaries and can handle the complete apostille process for international use.

How quickly can you deliver urgent legal translations?

We offer expedited services with same-day delivery for urgent documents under 2,000 words, and 24-hour delivery for documents up to 5,000 words. Additional fees apply for expedited services.

What qualifications do your legal translators have?

Our legal translators include attorneys, paralegals, and legal scholars with advanced degrees in law and translation. Many are certified by professional translation associations and have extensive experience in specific legal domains.
