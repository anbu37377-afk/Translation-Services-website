Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our pricing and billing

How do you calculate per-word pricing?

Per-word pricing is calculated based on the source word count. The rate varies depending on the language pair, content complexity, and required turnaround time. We provide detailed quotes before starting any project.

Are there any hidden fees or charges?

No, we believe in transparent pricing. The quote you receive includes all costs including translation, editing, proofreading, and quality assurance. Additional services like notarization or expedited delivery are clearly priced separately.

Can I change plans anytime?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your subscription plan at any time. Changes take effect at the beginning of the next billing cycle, and we'll prorate any differences.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, bank transfers, and ACH payments for enterprise clients. Subscription plans are billed monthly or annually, with discounts for annual billing.

Do you offer refunds?

We offer a satisfaction guarantee. If you're not satisfied with the quality of our translation, we'll work with you to make it right or provide a refund according to our refund policy.

How do volume discounts work?

Volume discounts are automatically applied based on your monthly or project word count. The more you translate, the higher the discount tier you qualify for. Discounts are cumulative and apply to all language pairs.
