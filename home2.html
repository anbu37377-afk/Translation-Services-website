About LinguaFlow

Founded by Translation Professionals

Founded by Translation Professionals

LinguaFlow was established in 2018 by a team of certified translators and linguists who recognized the growing need for professional, culturally-aware translation services in an increasingly globalized world. Our founders combined their expertise in linguistics, technology, and business to create a translation agency that prioritizes accuracy, cultural relevance, and client success.

What began as a small team of 5 translators serving local businesses has grown into a global network of 500+ native-speaking translators serving clients in 50+ countries. We've successfully completed over 100,000 translation projects while maintaining our commitment to quality and customer satisfaction.
